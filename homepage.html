Welcome New Students!

Starting college can feel overwhelming, but you're not alone! ATYOU is here with tips, advice, and resources to help first-year ATU students settle in, find their way, and thrive. Whether you're struggling to navigate the campus, get set up, make new friends, or simply adjusting to life at ATU, we've got you covered. Your college journey is just beginning, and ATYOU is here to help you make the most of it!

What is ATYou?


ATYOU is a project created by students, for students, with a special focus on supporting neurodivergent individuals. We know that stepping into college life can feel overwhelming, especially when you’re unsure what to expect. That’s why we built ATYOU: to make the transition into third-level education at ATU easier for everyone!
