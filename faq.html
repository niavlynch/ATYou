Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What time is the canteen open?

The canteen is open from 7:30 AM to 7:00 PM, Monday through Friday. On weekends, it operates from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM..

How do I get a locker?

Lockers are available for rent at the student services office. To get a locker, simply visit the office, fill out the form, and make the required payment. Locker rental is valid for the entire semester.

Where can I find the class schedule?

Class schedules are available on the student portal. You can log in with your student ID and password to access your personalized timetable.

How do I register for classes?

Class registration is done through the student portal. You can access it during the registration period, which is announced at the beginning of each semester. Make sure to check the portal for any important updates.

Is there parking available on campus?

Yes, there is parking available on campus. Parking permits can be purchased at the campus parking office. There are designated areas for student parking, and spaces are available on a first-come, first-served basis.

How can I contact my professors?

Professors' contact details are available on the student portal. You can email them directly or attend their office hours, which are listed in your course syllabus.
