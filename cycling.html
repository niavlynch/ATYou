Cycling

Cycling Information

Cycling is super accessible for all student on campus! There are multiple bike sheds around campus. It is recommended to get familiar with the roads around campus that you will be cycling on prior to attending college. There are no cycle paths, but there are bus and taxi lanes to and from campus that cyclists can use to avoid traffic.

You can sign up at the student union to get a key to access the locked bike sheds, that have security cameras installed and a roof over the bikes to guarantee safe keeping. But, if you are worried about losing your key or forgetting it at home, don’t worry, there are open bike racks than you can use without needing access, just make sure you bring your bike lock! Which is always attached to your bike, so it’s easier to remember than your key 😊
